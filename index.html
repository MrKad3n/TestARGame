ELEMENTAL RIFT

A WebXR mixed-reality game. Command fire, water and air with your bare hands.

Checking for WebXR…
Hand tracking — turn on Settings → Movement tracking → Hand tracking on your Quest.
  • Pinch thumb + index: cast your element
  • Fist: empower — fire gets bigger and hotter (red → yellow → blue → white), water warms up
  • Move your hand: sweep elements around — they keep their momentum, and you can cancel it by moving back the right amount
  • Pinch thumb + middle: cool water — cold enough and it freezes into floating ice
  • Pinch thumb + ring: switch element (fire / water / air)
  • Look at the back of your left hand and poke the ≡ button to open the menu: pick each hand's element and remap every gesture.
Controller buttons — Quest 2 / Touch controllers.
  • Trigger: cast your element (left controller = water, right = fire)
  • A / X: empower (bigger, hotter fire / warmer water)
  • Grip: pull your element toward where you aim. Moving the controller also pushes elements.
  • B / Y: cool water (cold enough and it freezes)
  • Thumbstick click: switch element
  • Point at the ≡ button above your left controller and pull the trigger to open the menu: pick each hand's element, remap buttons, or latch CAST / HEAT / PULL / COOL.
Goal
  • Frost wisps melt under fire. Ember imps are put out by water (ice hurts most).
  • Stone husks only crack under blue / white-hot fire — compress your flames. Soaking them with water first makes fire do far more.
  • Air gusts shove creatures back. Don't let them reach you!
Your room matters
  • Quest 3: elements bounce off your real walls, floor and furniture using your Space Setup room scan.
  • Quest 2: elements collide with the floor and your Guardian boundary.
Tip: WebXR needs HTTPS (or localhost). See README.md.
Mouse = hand. Hold Q / click to cast, Space empower, R redirect to the cursor, B cool water, E switch element, C compress, G pull in, V blast away, X stop momentum, Z dispel.
